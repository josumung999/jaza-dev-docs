Users top up in **local currency** via Mobile Money (and card where enabled). Enable markets in the [dashboard](https://jaza.dev/dashboard); Jaza collects across supported African markets and settles USD to you — you do **not** depend on your own aggregator merchant coverage.

## Markets (typical corridors)

Availability is controlled per app in the dashboard. Common corridors include:

| Currency | Code | Examples of rails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kenyan Shilling | KES | M-Pesa, card (where enabled) |
| Congolese Franc | CDF | Airtel Money |
| Ghanaian Cedi | GHS | MTN MoMo, card |
| Nigerian Naira | NGN | Mobile Money / card where enabled |
| Ugandan Shilling | UGX | MTN MoMo, Airtel Money |
| Rwandan Franc | RWF | MTN MoMo |
| Tanzanian Shilling | TZS | Airtel Money |

Confirm the live list for your app under **App settings → markets**. Contact support if you need a corridor that is not listed for your account.

## Credits vs currency

- Customers pay in local currency; wallets hold **credits**.
- Bundle prices and feature costs are configured in the dashboard.
- Merchant settlements convert to USD and pay out to your bank when you reach the **$100** threshold — see [Settlements](/concepts/settlements).

<Tip>
  You do not set up an aggregator account or maintain FX tables. Enable markets; Jaza runs collection and settlement.
</Tip>
