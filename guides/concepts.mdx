## Credits

Credits are the unit of prepaid balance on a customer's wallet. You price features in credits in the dashboard (for example, `SEND_MESSAGE` = 5 credits). Users buy packs (bundles) with Mobile Money; your backend debits credits with `consume`.

Internally Jaza maps credits to USD for analytics and settlement. You do not maintain FX tables or per-currency ledgers for MoMo collections.

## Customers and wallets

Each end user maps to a Jaza **customer** (`cus_…`) and a wallet scoped to your app and environment (sandbox or live). Create the customer once with `@jazadev/node` and store the id in your database.

## Sandbox vs live

| | Sandbox | Live |
|---|---------|------|
| Keys | `jz_test_sk_…` / `jz_test_pk_…` | `jz_live_sk_…` / `jz_live_pk_…` |
| Data | Isolated test wallets and payments | Production |
| Use for | Integration and QA | Real users |

Switch environments in the dashboard. Operational data (wallets, top-ups, consumption) is isolated per environment; app config like features and bundles is shared.

## Bundles and features

- **Bundles** — credit packs users buy in the top-up sheet (price + credits).
- **Features** — named actions with a `creditsCost`. Pass `featureCode` to `consume`.

Configure both in the dashboard before calling the SDKs.

## Mobile Money and markets

Enable countries / Mobile Money markets in **App Settings**. Jaza routes deposits through its payment partners (including PawaPay coverage across African markets).

<Note>
  You do **not** create or manage a PawaPay merchant account. Jaza is the merchant of record for collection rails; you integrate SDKs and enable markets in the dashboard.
</Note>

## Settlements

When users top up, Jaza collects local currency via Mobile Money, credits the user's wallet, and settles value to you according to your merchant settlement flow in the dashboard (USD-oriented balances and payouts).

You do not:

- Reconcile multi-currency MoMo deposits yourself
- Operate aggregator payouts country by country
- Build a second ledger for FX

You do:

- Track credit balances and consumption for product UX
- Use dashboard analytics for revenue and activity

## Top-up session

`jaza.topUp({ customerId })` creates a short-lived session and JWT. The React Native SDK uses that token (with your publishable key) to list bundles, quote, and deposit. Poll with `jaza.check({ topUpId })` on the server if you need session status outside the client sheet.
