When users top up, Jaza:

1. **Collects** local currency via Mobile Money across supported African markets (and card where enabled).
2. **Converts** into your unified credit model using customer FX rates (local units per USD).
3. **Accrues** USD to your settlement wallet after a **5% platform fee** on each top-up.
4. **Settles** to your bank when you request a payout or on your chosen schedule, with a **$100 USD** minimum.

## Platform fee

Jaza takes **5% of each top-up** from the merchant share. End users still pay the bundle price in local currency — the fee is not added on top of their MoMo charge.

Example: a **$10** bundle top-up credits **$9.50** to your settlement wallet.

## FX

Customer quotes use a **customer-pays** local-per-USD rate (the rate to buy $1 with local currency). A tighter treasury rate is kept for ops and margin; it is not charged to the end user.

## Bank account and frequency

In the dashboard **Payouts** page:

- Save one bank account (holder name, account number, bank, SWIFT/BIC, branch address, country, currency).
- Choose a settlement frequency: weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or quarterly.
- Request a settlement anytime when available balance is at least **$100 USD**.

After each completed settlement, Jaza emails an invoice PDF. Funds typically arrive in **2–5 business days**.

## What you do not do

- Open or operate a PawaPay (or similar) merchant account for collection.
- Maintain per-country FX tables for MoMo deposits.
- Build a second ledger for currency conversion.

## What you do

- Enable markets, features, and bundles in the dashboard.
- Integrate `@jazadev/node` plus `@jazadev/react-native` or `@jazadev/react`.
- Configure bank details and settlement frequency; use dashboard analytics and payouts for reporting.

## Sandbox vs live

Operational data (wallets, top-ups, consumption, settlement wallets) is isolated by environment. Use test keys until flows look right, then switch to live.
